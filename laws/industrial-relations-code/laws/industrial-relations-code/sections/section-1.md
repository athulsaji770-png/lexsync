# Section 2 — Definitions

Section 2 defines the core terms used throughout the Code, including:

- **Worker** — any person employed to do manual, unskilled, skilled, technical, operational, clerical or supervisory work for hire or reward (includes working journalists and sales promotion employees; excludes apprentices).
- **Industrial dispute** — any dispute between employers and workers connected with employment, terms of employment, or conditions of labour — including disputes arising from discharge, dismissal, retrenchment, or termination.
- **Lock-out** — temporary closing of a workplace, or an employer's refusal to continue employing workers.
- **Trade Union** — any combination formed to regulate relations between workers and employers, or between workers themselves.
- **Strike** (as amended by this Code) — includes mass casual leave: if over 50% of a company's workers take concerted casual leave together, it is treated as a strike.
